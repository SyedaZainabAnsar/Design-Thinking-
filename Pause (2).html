🏠 Home page user guide

Tap a topic to see what it does and how to use it.

🙂 Your avatar and name

The circle shows the first letter of your name, with your name beneath it. You enter your name once, when you first set up your account.

📈 Level, XP and goal

The line under your name shows your level, your total XP and your main goal. Earn XP by solving puzzles (+10), ticking habits (+5) and finishing offline ideas (+20).

📊 The XP bar

The red bar shows how close you are to the next level. It fills as you earn XP, and every 50 XP gives you a new level.

🧩 Brain Puzzles button

Opens pattern puzzles, reward badges and the friends leaderboard. Use it when you want a quick, fun challenge.

🎛️ Digital Control button

Opens your screen-time tools: daily goal, app limits and focus reminders, plus habit building and offline ideas. Use it when you want to cut back on scrolling.

✨ First-time setup

On your first visit you pick a name, a main goal, a daily screen-time goal and your interests. Tap Create my account to start. Your details are saved on your device, so you will not be asked again.

🧭 Using the other pages

What to do inside Brain Puzzles and Digital Control.

🧠 Solve a puzzle

Find the number that continues the pattern, then tap your answer. Each correct answer earns 10 XP.

🏆 Earn rewards

Badges unlock as your XP grows, and every 50 XP is a new level. Puzzles get harder as you level up.

👥 Compete with friends

See where you rank against your friends. Solve more puzzles to climb the leaderboard.

🎯 Set a daily goal

Drag the slider to choose how many minutes a day you want to stay under.

⏳ Limit single apps

Tap − or + to set a time limit for each app that eats your day.

🔔 Get focus reminders

Turn on reminders and pick how often. A gentle nudge tells you to look away and rest.

✅ Build habits

Tick a habit when you finish it to earn 5 XP. The dots show your streak. You can also add your own habit.

🌿 Do something offline

Swap a scroll for something real. Tap Mark as done on an idea to earn 20 XP, or ask for a surprise.
